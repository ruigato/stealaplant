# Steal a Plant 🌱

A Roblox game inspired by Steal a Brainrot, but with plants!

## Project Overview

**Steal a Plant** is a competitive Roblox game inspired by Steal a Brainrot where players can:
- **Claim bases** - Choose from 6 available bases in the map
- **Collect plants** - From your personal conveyor belt system
- **Steal from others** - Rob plants from other players' unlocked bases
- **Protect your base** - Lock/unlock strategically to prevent theft
- **Discover mutations** - Find Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Rainbow variants
- **Rebirth for power** - Reset progress for permanent bonuses
- **Participate in events** - Weekly YouTube-integrated admin events
- **Build collections** - Complete your plant index across 10 rarities

## Quick Info

## Key Features

### 🌱 Plant System
- **10 Rarity Tiers**: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → Plant God → Secret → Divine → **OG (Pastel de Nata)**
- **4 Mutation Types**: Silver, Gold, Diamond, Rainbow
- **Individual bases**: Each player has their own spawn trees and conveyor belt
- **6 bases per server**: Limited, competitive environment

### ⚔️ PvP Mechanics
- **Steal from others**: Enter unlocked bases and rob plants
- **Base locking**: Strategic lock/unlock to protect your collection
- **Risk vs Reward**: Balance farming your own plants vs stealing

### 🔄 Progression
- **Rebirth System**: Reset for permanent multipliers
- **Money Generation**: Plants earn money per second
- **Base Expansion**: Buy more flower pot slots
- **Collection Goals**: Complete your plant index

### 🎉 Community Events
- **Every Saturday 18:00** (Portugal time)
