# Steal a Plant - Game Design Document

## Overview

**Steal a Plant** is a Roblox idle/collecting game where players purchase plants from a conveyor belt system, collect them in their base, and earn money over time. The game features a mutation system, various rarities, and weekly community events.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Plants spawn** from two trees and fall onto a grass conveyor belt
2. **Players purchase plants** from the conveyor belt
3. **Purchased plants** go to the player's index (collection)
4. **Plants generate money** over time based on rarity and mutations
5. **Unpurchased plants** are collected by a truck at the end of the belt
6. **Players use money** to buy more/better plants and expand their collection

## Game World

### Spawn Area
- **Two large trees** at the spawn point
- Plants fall from the trees onto the conveyor belt below
- Natural, garden-like atmosphere

### Conveyor Belt System
- **Material**: Grass (instead of traditional metal/rubber)
- Plants travel along the grass conveyor to the end
- Multiple players can purchase from the same belt

### Player Base
- **Material**: Wooden base with flower details/decorations
- **Storage Slots**: Flower pots (vasos) where plants are placed
- Players can expand their base for more plant slots
- Each pot can hold one plant

### Truck System
- **Location**: End of the conveyor belt
- **Function**: Collects unpurchased plants
- **Capacity**: 1,000 plants
- **Behavior**: When full, truck leaves and a new empty truck arrives immediately

## Player Progression

### Index System
- Player's personal collection of all plants they own
- Shows:
  - Plant name and rarity
  - Mutation status (if any)
  - Money earned per second
  - Total owned of each type

### Economy
- Plants generate money per second
- Money is used to purchase more plants
- Mutation status affects plant value and earnings
- (Specific pricing and earnings rates in development)

## Visual Style

### Art Direction
- Hand-drawn plant designs created by the development team
- Colorful, friendly aesthetic
- Portuguese influences
- Garden/nature theme throughout

### Base Aesthetics
- **Different from Steal a Brainrot**: Custom wooden design
- Flower decorations and details
- Flower pots as plant containers
- Natural, organic look

## Inspiration

Based on **Steal a Brainrot** but with original mechanics and theme:
- Same core conveyor belt concept
- Different visual style and theme
- Custom base designs
- Unique plant-based content

## Target Audience

- **Primary**: Kids and teens (10-15 years old)
- **Region**: Portugal (but playable worldwide)
- **Language**: English (for international reach)
- **Interest**: Collecting games, idle games, Roblox community

## Unique Selling Points

1. **Portuguese Origin**: Made by Portuguese students
2. **Hand-Drawn Plants**: Original art from the team
3. **Mutation System**: Rare plant variants to discover
4. **Weekly Events**: Community engagement through YouTube
5. **OG Rarity**: Special "Pastel de Nata" plant (Portuguese cultural reference)
6. **Custom Aesthetics**: Unique wooden bases with flower details

## Future Considerations

- Trading system between players
- Special seasonal plants
- Base customization options
- Leaderboards
- Achievement system
- Plant fusion/combination mechanics
